# Pittsburgh dealer prospects

Research draft. No one has been contacted.

Companion data file: `dealer-prospects-pittsburgh.csv` (9 rows, PIT-D-01 to PIT-D-09). Market: City of Pittsburgh and Allegheny County. The angle is lead service lines and general filtration, so rows with filtration, RO or lead work were preferred.

## Method

- **Date:** 2026-10-28. Read-only public web research with web search and page fetches only. No forms, accounts, calls, emails, people-search or enrichment services.
- **Manufacturer pages tried:**
  - Culligan: culliganpittsburgh.com redirects to Culligan's Pittsburgh location page, which loaded.
  - Kinetico dealer pages that loaded (Clearwater Systems, Gordon Brothers Water, Aquarius, Huemann) do not name Pittsburgh or Allegheny County.
  - No RainSoft, EcoWater or Hague dealer page for Pittsburgh was found before the search budget ran out.
- **Other sources:** web searches for Pittsburgh and Allegheny County water treatment, softeners, RO, and plumbers with filtration and lead service line work.
- **What loaded and what did not:** the shared web search budget for this run ran out early in Pittsburgh. Independents, brand dealers and suburb-by-suburb searches could not be run further. wellnesswaterfiltrationsystemspittsburghpa.com refused automated access.
- **What a row needed:** the company's own site confirming residential filtration, RO or softener installs and naming Pittsburgh or Allegheny County.

## Licensing finding

- **Regulator:** Allegheny County Health Department (ACHD), Plumbing Program. Plumbing in Pittsburgh and Allegheny County runs under the ACHD Plumbing Code (Article XV).
  - The July 2025 Article XV revision (https://www.alleghenycounty.us/files/assets/county/v/1/government/health/documents/article-xv-final-7-10-2025.pdf) says no one other than a licensed master plumber, a licensed journeyman or a registered apprentice working for one, or an owner-occupant of a single-family home, "shall install, alter, or make connections with any sewer, water, drain, or any pipe connected therewith" (AC-106.5.8). This is the proposed revision text, and some struck language may show as current.
  - Section 608.4 of the same document covers "filters, softeners, tanks and other appliances and devices that handle or treat potable water", including air gaps on their discharge.
  - ACHD's permit page (https://www.alleghenycounty.us/Services/Health-Department/Plumbing-Program/Project-Plans-and-Permitting) says residential plumbing projects need a plumbing permit.
- **No separate water treatment license** was found. My reading, not an ACHD ruling, is that connecting a softener or whole-house filter to the water line needs an ACHD-licensed master plumber and permit.
- **Lookup:** ACHD's Master Plumber Search (webapps.achd.net/Plumbing/) and the WPRDC "Allegheny County Plumbers" data (https://data.wprdc.org/dataset/allegheny-county-plumbers) both refused automated access (robots). WPRDC also notes that updates were suspended on 07/29/2026. No license was verified.
- Pennsylvania Home Improvement Contractor registration was not checked.
- **Self-reported numbers:** Wahl PA Contractor #22302 and Plumbing #04202; Taggart #PA126149; Revelation PA185386 and HP: 5066.

## Top three to consider

1. **PIT-D-04 WAHL Family Heating, Cooling & Plumbing:** it installs whole-home filtration, RO and softeners across Allegheny County every week and also replaces lead service lines, which fits the Pittsburgh lead angle best.
2. **PIT-D-01 Aqua Solutions:** it is a full-time water treatment specialist since 1997 with a named owner and a Pittsburgh service page, though its office is in Butler.
3. **PIT-D-05 WJ McNabb Plumbing:** it names its co-owners, one a master plumber, and states professional installation across all Pittsburgh ZIP codes for whole-house, RO and softener systems.

No license could be verified for any row, because ACHD's lookup and the county data both refused automated access.

## Gaps

- **Row count is 9, below the 12 to 15 target.** The search budget ran out before more independents and brand dealers (RainSoft, EcoWater, Hague, Kinetico) could be found.
- **Licenses:** none verified. The ACHD lookup and WPRDC data were blocked. PA HIC not checked.
- **Lead claims:** no row states an NSF/ANSI standard. Sullivan and Taggart claim lead reduction in general terms. Wahl offers lead line replacement rather than a lead filter claim.
- **Decision makers:** named only for Aqua Solutions and McNabb.
- **Missing details:** no street address for Cesare's, Sullivan or the Culligan page. Culligan shows only a national number. Revelation's phone shows as a placeholder. Email published only by Cesare's and Revelation.
- **Reviews:** not opened.
