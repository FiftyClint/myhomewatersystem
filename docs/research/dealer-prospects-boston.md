# Boston dealer prospects

Research draft. No one has been contacted.

Companion data file: `dealer-prospects-boston.csv` (14 rows, BOS-D-01 to BOS-D-14). Market: Boston and the inner MWRA suburbs (Cambridge, Somerville, Quincy, Newton, Brookline and nearby). The angle is lead service lines and general filtration, so rows with filtration, RO or lead claims were preferred.

## Method

- **Date:** 2026-10-28. Read-only public web research with web search and page fetches only. No forms, accounts, calls, emails, people-search or enrichment services.
- **Manufacturer pages tried:**
  - Culligan: two dealers' own sites loaded (Culligan Water Filtration of New England, and Culligan Water of Massachusetts in Lowell).
  - Kinetico: Premier Water Systems (Walpole) states on its own site that it is an authorized, independent Kinetico dealer. Kinetico dealer pages found by search were out of state.
  - The Hague Massachusetts dealer page loaded with no dealer data.
  - RainSoft of Boston (Hudson) loaded. No EcoWater dealer page for the Boston core was found; High Purity Water uses EcoWater RO units.
- **Other sources:** web searches by town and neighborhood (Dorchester, Jamaica Plain, Brookline, Woburn, Medford, Braintree, Waltham), plus other wordings (lead, chloramine, MWRA, family owned, plumber).
- **What a row needed:** the company's own site confirming residential filtration, RO or softener work and naming Boston, an inner MWRA town, or a county that contains them.

## Licensing finding

- **Regulator:** Massachusetts Board of State Examiners of Plumbers and Gas Fitters, within the Division of Occupational Licensure.
  - The Uniform State Plumbing Code (248 CMR 10.00, https://www.mass.gov/doc/248-cmr-1000-uniform-state-plumbing-code-0/download) defines a water softener and a water filter as devices "installed on a potable water system". It also says the plumbing exemptions for pipes "apply to pipes only", not to appliances connected to them.
  - 248 CMR 3.00 (https://www.mass.gov/doc/plgf-regulation-filing-248-cmr-3-red-lined-2025/download) requires a permit before plumbing work. Its short list of permit exceptions does not include water treatment devices.
- **No separate water treatment license** was found. My reading, not a Board ruling, is that installing a softener or whole-house filter is plumbing work needing a licensed plumber and a local permit.
- **Lookup:** the state check-a-license page (https://www.mass.gov/how-to/check-an-occupational-board-license) points to the eLIPSE platform (https://occupationallicensingandpermitting.mass.gov/madol/s/). It loads by JavaScript and returned only a loading/error placeholder, so the lookup was blocked. No license was verified.
- **Self-reported numbers** are in each row's notes: Premier 4731-PL-C; Boston Standard/Sila plumbing 4413-C, HIC 199117, ML#15216; Endless Energy plumbing 4926, HIC 202202; Boston Tank Swap 16763-PL-M and 9629366-PL-C; Johnny Rocket 9635366-PL-PC. Massachusetts HIC registration was not checked.

## Top three to consider

1. **BOS-D-02 ThinkPure Water:** it is a filtration specialist with a stated NSF 53 lead claim, shows an install in Newton, covers Suffolk and Middlesex counties, and publishes a sales email.
2. **BOS-D-01 H2O Care:** it is an independent, not-a-franchise water treatment company whose own master and journeyman plumbers do the installs, which fits Massachusetts' plumbing rules, and it covers the counties that hold the MWRA core.
3. **BOS-D-05 Premier Water Systems:** it is an authorized Kinetico dealer with a dedicated Boston page that names Quincy and Brookline, says it installs, and prints a plumbing license number.

None of these licenses could be verified, because the state lookup did not load.

## Gaps

- **Licenses:** no license verified. The eLIPSE lookup is JavaScript-only. All numbers are self-reported. HIC registration not checked.
- **Coverage:** H2O Care, ThinkPure and High Purity Water name counties or "Greater Boston" more than towns. RainSoft of Boston names no town. Culligan Water of Massachusetts does not list Boston or Brookline. CleanTap's main page does not name Cambridge, Somerville, Newton or Brookline.
- **Lead claims:** only ThinkPure states a standard (NSF 53, component wording). Boston Standard and High Purity Water claim lead removal without a standard.
- **Distance:** High Purity Water (Sutton), Culligan of New England (Sandwich) and RainSoft (Hudson) are well outside the city.
- **Decision makers:** no owner is named on any company site. H2O Care names a President.
- **Missing details:** no email for H2O Care, High Purity, CleanTap, Premier, RainSoft, both Culligans, Boston Standard or Johnny Rocket. No street address for ThinkPure, Boston Tank Swap or Johnny Rocket.
- **Reviews:** not opened.

## Exclusions

- **Cambridge Plumbing Co (cambridgeplumbingco.com):** no address, and it links service pages for Springfield, Worcester, New Bedford, Lowell and Leominster. Looks like a lead-generation template.
- **PureSpring Water Softeners & Filters Boston:** same template network as the PureSpring Staten Island site. Treated as lead generation.
- **Sila Boston page and Central Cooling & Heating (Woburn):** they print the same HIC and plumbing numbers as Boston Standard, so they are folded into BOS-D-09 rather than listed separately.
- **Friot's Water Treatment (Ayer):** its own town list names no Boston or MWRA core town.
- **Olde Colony Clean Water / SafeWell (Bolton):** the Woburn page lists no Boston core town. The SafeWell Boston page uses a toll-free number and shares a Florida address. Not confirmed as a local Boston installer.
- **Horvitz Plumbing & Heating:** serves Boston, but its page lists no water filtration, softener or RO service.
- **Commonwealth Water Treatment:** a Virginia Kinetico dealer, not Massachusetts.
- **M&S Plumbing:** Manhattan, Kansas.
- **Boston Budget Plumbing, abostonplumber.com, south-end-plumbing.com, maxrooter.us, bostonplumbinginc.com:** search results with lead-generation style domains. Not opened as evidence.
