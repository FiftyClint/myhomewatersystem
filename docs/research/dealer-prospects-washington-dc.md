# Washington DC dealer prospects

Research draft. No one has been contacted.

Companion data file: `dealer-prospects-washington-dc.csv` (5 rows, DC-D-01 to DC-D-05). Market: Washington, District of Columbia (District ZIPs). Dealers based in Maryland or Virginia are included only where their own site says they serve DC.

## Method

- **Date:** 2026-10-28. Read-only public web research with web search and page fetches only. No forms, accounts, calls, emails, people-search or enrichment services were used.
- **Manufacturer locators tried:**
  - Culligan: all four pages of the culliganwater.com locations archive loaded; none listed a Maryland, Virginia or DC dealer. The ZIP-based "Set My Location" tool was not used.
  - EcoWater: the expert.ecowater.com city pages for Washington DC and Rockville MD returned errors.
  - Hague: Hague Quality Water of Maryland has its own DC service page (included).
  - Kinetico and RainSoft: not reached before the search budget ran out.
- **Other sources:** web search for softener, RO and filtration installers in DC, and the HomeAdvisor DC list (names only). Yelp refused automated access (robots). The Porch list returned no business names.
- **Search budget:** the shared web search limit for this session ran out early in the DC work, after the first DC search returned. That is why this market has only 5 rows. Names found but not researched are listed under Gaps.
- **What a row needed:** each row was confirmed on the company's own website, which had to show water treatment sales and installation and say that it serves Washington DC. Every field comes from a page that was opened, listed in `contact_source_url`.

## Licensing finding

- **Regulator:** the DC Department of Licensing and Consumer Protection (DLCP), through the Board of Industrial Trades, licenses plumbers and gasfitters at apprentice, journeyman and master level, and licenses trade contractors. Licenses expire March 31 of even-numbered years. Sources: https://dlcp.dc.gov/node/1619081 and https://dlcp.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/DLCP/publication/attachments/PlumbingGasfitting.pdf
- **Contractor license:** a plumbing/gasfitting trade contractor needs a $5,000 company bond and a $5,000 master bond, a "Designated DC-licensed master tradesperson" who supervises the work, and registration in good standing with the DC Corporations Division. Source: https://dlcp.dc.gov/sites/default/files/dc/sites/DLCP/publication/attachments/Trade%20Contractor.pdf
- **Water treatment:** the DLCP pages opened do not mention softeners, filtration or water treatment. The DC plumbing code text was not checked.
- **What this means in practice (inference):** installing a softener, RO unit or whole-house filter ties into supply piping, so a DC partner should hold a DC plumbing trade contractor license with a DC master plumber, or use one. DLCP also posted a notice of new licensure reciprocity for Maryland plumbing professionals, which may matter for Maryland-based dealers.
- **Lookup:** the DLCP occupational license verification portal (https://govservices.dcra.dc.gov/oplaportal) refused automated access (robots). No license was verified. F.H. Furr shows "DC Plumbing: PGM1000925" on its site (self-reported). Maryland and Virginia lookups were not tried.
