# Dealer scorecard and discovery kit

Draft. The weights below are the plan's proposed weights, not a validated model. A high score never overrides a failed gate.

## Mandatory gates (any "no" = not eligible)
- [ ] Plumbing license for installs in each city served (Johnson County, KCMO, etc.) held by the dealer or a named subcontracted plumber. Dealer sends the copy. Licensing is set city by city in KS and MO (see kc-licensing.md)
- [ ] Current insurance certificate verified
- [ ] Agrees to report outcomes per lead (accepted, booked, held, quoted, installed, paid)
- [ ] Owns customer service, warranty and urgent leak response, with a named urgent contact
- [ ] Signed agreement
- [ ] Real weekly capacity stated in writing

## Discovery questions (from the plan)
1. Which products and homes do you want, and which do you turn down?
2. Which ZIP codes can you serve, and how many new assessments a week can you take?
3. Who calls new leads, how fast, and who covers when they are out?
4. How do you handle water testing, sizing and non-standard installs?
5. What is your written offer, what is excluded, and what is the maintenance plan and warranty?
6. Who holds the licenses and insurance, and can we verify them?
7. What acquisition cost per sale can your margins support?
8. Which fee model would you accept: you fund ads plus a service fee, a fee per held appointment, or a fee per paid install? When is it earned and paid?
9. Will you report status on each lead using a link we send?
10. Any manufacturer or franchise rules on territory, branding, referrals or advertising?
11. Who handles a leak call at night?
12. May we speak with two references, with their permission?

## Ask them to send before a decision
Business and license documents, service ZIPs, standard install scope and exclusions, offer and model numbers, sample customer contract and warranty, insurance certificate, a sample status report, weekly capacity, urgent contact, and a written fee proposal. Redacted samples only; no customer data.
