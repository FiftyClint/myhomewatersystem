## Method

- **Date:** 2026-10-28. Read-only public web research with web search and page fetches only. No forms, accounts, calls, emails, people-search or enrichment services were used.
- **Manufacturer locators tried:**
  - Kinetico: the static dealer page for Water Doctors (Waukesha) loaded and gave its service region.
  - Culligan: the Culligan Water of Waukesha dealer page loaded and lists Milwaukee. Culligan of West Bend and Culligan of Burlington appeared in BBB lists but were not checked for Milwaukee coverage.
  - Pentair True Blue: a static page loaded for Butler Soft Water LLC (Brookfield). Its nearby-dealer search showed "No dealers found".
  - RainSoft: the Milwaukee pages on rainsoft.com name no dealer. RainSoft of Wisconsin was found by search and opened (see exclusions).
  - EcoWater: search found only dealers far from Milwaukee (Two Rivers, Monroe).
  - Hague: no Milwaukee dealer page found; S&K Pump & Plumbing says on its own site that it is a Hague dealer.
- **Other sources:** web search by product and suburb (Waukesha, West Allis, Wauwatosa, Oak Creek, Franklin, Menomonee Falls, Mequon), BBB category lists for Milwaukee, Brookfield, Waukesha and Oak Creek, and the Angi Milwaukee list. BBB and Angi were used only to find names. SuperPages returned no listings. Yelp was not tried.
- **Search budget:** the shared web search limit for this session ran out near the end of the work. Clean Water Center (Oconomowoc, Water-Right), Benjamin Franklin Plumbing of Southeastern Wisconsin, Bri-An PHC, Proven Plumbing and C&V Plumbing were found as names but not researched.
- **What a row needed:** each row was confirmed on the company's own website, which had to show water treatment sales and installation and an area that reaches the market. Every field comes from a page that was opened, listed in `contact_source_url`.

## Licensing finding

- **State law:** Wisconsin licenses plumbing through the Department of Safety and Professional Services (DSPS). Wis. Stat. 145.14 sets up restricted plumber licenses with an "appliances, equipment and devices" classification. Under 145.14(2)(b), people installing "a water treatment device, a water heater, or any other item in connection with a water distribution system" that does not need a direct drain connection "are limited to making connections to existing installations", with "no drilling, tapping, or direct connection made to any waste or drain pipe". Source: https://law.justia.com/codes/wisconsin/chapter-145/section-145-14/
- **Credential:** DSPS issues the Master Plumber Restricted Appliance license, with Journeyman Plumber Restricted Appliance and Plumbing Learner Restricted Appliance below it. The DSPS page says the scope is installing "a water treatment device, a water heater, or any other item in connection with a water distribution system." Source: https://dsps.wi.gov/Pages/Professions/MasterPlumberRestrictedAppliance/Default.aspx
- **DSPS appliance FAQ:** a plumbing license or registration is required for an appliance that connects to a water supply or drainage system. It lists the restricted appliance credentials as acceptable, and says the individual must be "under the allowable direction of a State of Wisconsin licensed Master Plumber." Source: https://dsps.wi.gov/Documents/Programs/Plumbing/FAQPlumbingApplianceInstallation.pdf
- **What this means in practice (inference):** a pilot partner should hold a Master Plumber or Master Plumber Restricted Appliance credential, or work under one. Neither DSPS page names softeners specifically, so whether a softener with a drain line fits the restricted class or needs a full plumbing license was not confirmed. City of Milwaukee permit rules were not checked.
- **Lookup:** the DSPS Trades Credential/License Search (https://app.wi.gov/licensesearch) refused automated access (robots). No license was verified. Z PLUMBERZ shows "WI License 1213 - MP" on its site; that is recorded as self-reported.

## Top three to consider

1. **MKE-D-01 Zeropoint Mfg. Co., Inc.:** it is the only water treatment specialist found with an office inside the City of Milwaukee, it installs softeners, iron filters and RO, and it publishes a business email and several branch phones.
2. **MKE-D-02 Hanson Soft Water, Inc.:** it is a softener and RO specialist whose own service list names Milwaukee ZIPs and nearly every Milwaukee County suburb, and it publishes a business email.
3. **MKE-D-03 Soft Water, Inc.:** it is a specialist that names Milwaukee in its service area and installs softeners, iron filters and RO, with plumbing service in house.

Note: no row has a verified license, so none of the three meets the "verified license" test yet.

## Gaps

- **No license verified.** The DSPS lookup refused automated access. A manual search of the DSPS tool would settle all rows; Z PLUMBERZ (MKE-D-12) is the only row that shows a number.
- **Legal entity** is shown on site for 5 of 12 rows. **Owner** is not named on any company site. Guthrie & Frey's owners are named only in a news article.
- **Locators not captured:** RainSoft, EcoWater, Hague and Pentair dealer searches need a ZIP entry in a browser.
- **Unresearched names** because the search budget ran out: Clean Water Center (Oconomowoc), Benjamin Franklin Plumbing of Southeastern Wisconsin (Mt. Pleasant), Bri-An PHC (Waukesha), Proven Plumbing (Muskego), C&V Plumbing (Germantown), Culligan of West Bend, Culligan of Burlington.
- **Market note (inference):** most specialists sit in Waukesha County, where well and hard-water demand is high. Milwaukee itself is on Lake Michigan water, so city demand may lean toward lead and RO filtration over softeners.
- **Reviews:** not opened.
