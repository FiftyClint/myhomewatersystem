Research draft. No one has been contacted.

Market: `denver` (City and County of Denver plus inner suburbs served by Denver Water, such as Lakewood, Englewood, Littleton and Wheat Ridge). Companion data file: `dealer-prospects-denver.csv` (13 rows, DEN-D-01 to DEN-D-13). Six companies also appear on the Aurora list (`dealer-prospects-aurora.csv`, not edited); they are listed here only because their own site names Denver or a Denver inner suburb, and each row says so in notes.

## Method

- **Date:** 2026-10-28. Read-only public web research with web search and page fetches. No forms, accounts, calls, emails, people-search or enrichment services.
- **Homeowner concern:** lead service lines and general filtration, so rows with RO, whole-house filtration or lead content on their own site were favored. No row's own site states an NSF/ANSI 53 or 58 certification. Three show a generic "NSF Certified" or "ANSI/NSF" claim with no standard number (DEN-D-05, DEN-D-06, and the HALO line at DEN-D-10).
- **Brand pages that loaded:** Kinetico Advanced Water Systems' Denver location page and its companion site; Culligan of Denver's own site and the culligan.com Englewood dealer page; EcoWater's Colorado dealer page (Colorado Springs only). No RainSoft or EcoWater dealer for Denver was found.
- **Did not load:** denverwatersoftenercompany.com (site error). The DORA lookup page itself was not used; the state open-data copy of DORA records answered read-only queries instead.
- **Name finding only (never evidence):** web search results and the HBA Denver member listing.
- **Search wordings:** water treatment, softener, RO and lead filtration for Denver; softener and RO installation for Littleton and Englewood; Lakewood water treatment; brand searches for Kinetico, Culligan, EcoWater, Aerus and Hague.
- **What a row needed:** the company's own site confirming it sells and installs or services residential softeners, RO or whole-home filtration, and naming Denver, Denver neighborhoods or a Denver Water inner suburb.
- **Left blank on purpose:** review fields and score. Decision maker only where the company's own site names an owner (one row).

## Licensing finding

- **Which license applies.** Colorado regulates water conditioning work under the Plumbing Practice Act. C.R.S. 12-155-108 (read at https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_12-155-108) says a business doing water conditioning work must register with the State Plumbing Board as a **water conditioning contractor** with a registered **water conditioning principal**, and that **water conditioning installers** must register with proof of certification from a recognized national water conditioning association. Licensed plumbers, plumbing apprentices and registered plumbing contractors may do water conditioning work within their scope without those registrations.
- **Who runs it.** The Colorado State Plumbing Board in DORA's Division of Professions and Occupations.
- **Lookup.** The state open-data portal publishes DORA's license records with a link back to DORA's own verify page for each record: https://data.colorado.gov/Regulations/Professional-and-Occupational-Licenses-in-Colorado/7s5z-vewr . Every license number and status in the CSV comes from read-only queries of that dataset on 2026-10-28. The dataset does not state its refresh date.
- **What the records show.** The dataset holds 20 water conditioning contractor (WC) registrations statewide. None of the 13 rows holds one; each verified row is a plumbing contractor (PC) registration. Crystal Clear Water Treatment (Lakewood) holds a WC registration that expired 2025-02-28 and was not listed.
- **Local layer.** Denver's own contractor licensing and permit rules were not researched.
- **Result:** 10 of 13 rows have an Active PC registration from the DORA records (DEN-D-01, -03, -05, -06, -08 to -13). DEN-D-02, -04 and -07 were not found by name. Four rows carry discipline entries (DEN-D-01, -03, -09, -11; listed by case number only, details not opened). `license_checked_on` is 2026-10-28 on every row.

## Top three to consider

1. **DEN-D-01 Water Pros.** It is an owner-run water treatment specialist with a Denver page, a wide RO line that it says reduces trace lead, a published email and an active plumbing contractor registration, though the owner should be asked about the 2026 stipulation on the DORA record.
2. **DEN-D-05 Kinetico Advanced Water Systems.** It has a showroom in Denver, sells RO and a chloramine reduction system, and holds an active plumbing contractor registration with no discipline entry, though it publishes no email and its brand agreement may limit territory.
3. **DEN-D-06 Culligan of Denver.** It covers Lakewood and Littleton, sells whole-house RO and PFAS filters, publishes an email and holds an active plumbing contractor registration with no discipline entry, though as a Culligan franchise its territory and lead terms may be set by the brand.

Alternate: DEN-D-02 Colorado Water Purification writes to Denver neighborhoods by name and covers lead from older plumbing, but no license was found under its name.

## Gaps

- **Inner suburbs.** Wheat Ridge and Lakewood are named by only a few rows (DEN-D-03, -05, -06, -08, -10, -13). No specialist is based in Lakewood.
- **No NSF/ANSI 53 or 58 claims** on any row's own pages; lead claims are general.
- **Licenses not found:** DEN-D-02, -04 and -07 by name. A business can trade under a name different from its DORA record, so this is not proof they are unlicensed.
- **Discipline entries** on DEN-D-01, -03, -09 and -11 were not opened. DEN-D-03 and DEN-D-09 each include a formal complaint filed with the Office of Administrative Courts.
- **No street address on own site:** DEN-D-04 (Longmont only), DEN-D-10 and DEN-D-13 (city and ZIP only).
- **Emails:** published only for DEN-D-01, -02, -06 and -07. DEN-D-01's is the owner's name on the company domain.
- **Decision makers:** only DEN-D-01 names an owner.
- **Overlap with Aurora:** DEN-D-01, -02, -03, -07, -11 and -12 also appear on the Aurora list. Picking one of them for both cities would need a check on capacity (judgment call).
