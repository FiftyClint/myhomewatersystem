# Lead form consent wording (approved by Clint)

Status: approved by Clint on 2026-10-26 (no counsel review, his call). On 2026-10-28 he chose calls and email only in Texas until question 4 is settled, so Texas markets show no text box and their service consent names calls only. Policy `disclosure_version` is `owner-approved-2026-10-28`. Any change to the wording needs a new version and his approval again.

The live text is generated by `src/intake/disclosure.ts` so the stored consent record always matches what was shown. Three versions exist.

## 1. Dealer market, dealer named (what a homeowner sees once a dealer signs)
By checking this box and submitting, you ask My Home Water System, operated by Aidren LLC, to share your request with one local dealer, [Dealer], and you agree that [Dealer] and My Home Water System may contact you about this request by the method you chose, including calls or texts to the number you gave, which may use automated technology. [Dealer] provides your estimate, contract, installation and ongoing service. We will not share your request with any other company without asking you first. Consent is not required to buy anything.

## 2. Dealer market, no dealer signed yet (Ryan takes the call)
By checking this box and submitting, you ask My Home Water System, operated by Aidren LLC, to contact you about this request by the method you chose, including calls or texts to the number you gave, which may use automated technology. No local dealer is named for your area yet. Before we share your request with a dealer, we will tell you the dealer's name and ask you first. We will never share your request with more than one dealer. Consent is not required to buy anything.

## 3. Optional text message box (separate checkbox, not required)
Optional: I agree to receive text messages from My Home Water System (Aidren LLC) [and Dealer] about this request at the number I gave, which may be sent with automated technology. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not required to buy anything.

## How the system enforces the wording
- Consent is required to submit. The text box is optional and separate.
- Each consent record stores the exact text hash, version, named parties, time and page.
- A dealer who releases a lead puts it in `consent_hold`; it cannot go to another dealer without new consent.
